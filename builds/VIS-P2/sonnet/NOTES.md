# NOTES (VIS-P2, Kestrel Hand Pump)

**Assumptions**
- The brief gives no bracket, valve-block width or cage sizes. I chose them: block 92 wide, cage and passage sizes, and a gusseted bracket clamped to the barrel.
- The inlet ball sits in a pocket cut below the chamber floor so that the piston's 4 mm clearance is respected.
- Item 6's wording is shortened ("OD 72", "at bottom") to fit two lines at 12 units.
- Solid rods, pins and web plates are not hatched (drafting convention). Hatch directions alternate between adjacent cut parts.
- The stroke dimension is drawn on piston top faces, with extension lines passing the barrel wall.
- The plan key view omits items 10 and 11.

**Checked** (Chrome headless, own screenshots): renders at 1680x1188 and 1280x720; no external references; 40 KB; printed dimension lengths computed from the generator; table text fits its cells.
