Clear Skies Ledger

Ember Dunes has the most clear nights (298 a year) at only 420 m, while the highest site, Ninefold Mesa (2410 m), falls to 8 or 9 a month in July and August.

A clear night is one with under 20 percent cloud cover.  ·  Bortle class runs from 1 (darkest sky) to 9 (brightest).  ·  Eight observing sites, twelve months.Synthetic data prepared for this benchmark

How to explore

  • Hover a value, or move to it with the arrow keys, for its details.
  • Click a value, or press Enter, to pin it. Pin two to compare them.
  • Click a site name to isolate its row; click it again, or the background, to clear.
  • Hover a month to highlight its column.
  • Esc clears focus and pins.
