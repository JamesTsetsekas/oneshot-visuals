# Ripple Rail

The rail starts at rest. The hint disappears after the first successful grab, including when a grab happens while paused. The velocity legend saturates at ±3 rad/s, and the angle strip saturates at ±80°.

Verified in headless Chrome on Windows. Mouse and emulated touch drags, keyboard shortcuts, and button actions worked. At 1600×900, undamped energy read 9.31 throughout a 30.4-second sample. Fixed and Free results agreed with a smaller-step reference. Disk, HTTP root, and HTTP subpath produced identical rest screenshots with no resource requests. Four viewport sizes showed no scrollbars, and the console stayed clear.

End bobs can leave the stage during large outward swings. Physical pen and touch hardware and the specified laptop-class GPU were not verified.
